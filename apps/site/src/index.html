Features

Everything a decade of photos needs

Phones mix the moments you care about with screenshots, memes, receipts and the same photo five times. Roll pulls them apart and puts each one where it belongs.

Sorts itself

Rules, file signals and models on your computer put every item in a category: photos, screenshots, memes, social media saves, wallpapers, documents, junk. Only the doubtful rest waits for you, and you review it with the keyboard.

A real archive on disk

2023/Photos, 2023/2023-07 Lake Garda, 2023/Screenshots. Plain folders that open without Roll.

Fixes camera clocks

A camera that was a year and two hours off? One reference photo shifts the whole set, and phone photos of the same moments suggest the offset.

Finds the places

From Google Timeline and GPX history, your own geotagged photos taken at the same time, and folder names. Or drop items on the map.

People

Faces grouped by who they look like. Names go into the files as face regions, so Immich and other apps know them too.

Duplicates and best shots

Exact copies, the WhatsApp copy of a camera photo, look-alikes and bursts. The sharpest frame wins, and frames with closed eyes rank last.

Search by what's in it

"dog on a beach", "receipt", "summer 2019 italy". Roll knows what pictures show and reads the text in screenshots and documents.

Events

Trips and busy days are found for you. Confirm them and each gets its own folder and an Immich album.

Imports that know their source

Camera cards with their clock checked, WhatsApp chats with the time each file was sent, Google Takeout with its dates, places and albums.

Undo anything

Every edit, date fix, organize run and import is one step in History. Undo puts files and values back. Nothing is deleted for good: Roll's own trash keeps items on disk until you empty it.

Review

Sort 1,000 items before your coffee is cold

Review shows what Roll is least sure about first, with the reason under each item. One key picks a category, Enter accepts the page, and “Accept all at 95% or higher” takes the rest. A small audit keeps an eye on precision.

  • 1–9 pick a category
  • Enter accept the page
  • X skip
  • Z undo

Dates

One photo fixes a whole camera

Roll spots camera sets whose clock was never set, whose year disagrees with the folder, or that have no time zone. It compares them with phone photos of the same moments and proposes the offset. Pick a reference photo, enter its true time, and the preview shows which items change year before anything is saved.

Places

Every photo back on the map

Import your location history and Roll matches it to the photos taken along the way. Photos without GPS get the place of your own geotagged photos from the same moments, or of the place a folder is named after. Accept, adjust or reject each group, then set the time zones in one go.

Map

From the globe down to the building

A minimal globe with a time slider: drag the window through the years, press play, and watch where you were. Then zoom in until you can tell your university's main building from the one next door.

Works offline

Country outlines are built in. Download the world map (63 MB) for lakes, rivers, main roads and cities everywhere.

Every building where it matters

Keep the places you photograph offline, down to every building and footpath. Central Lisbon is 18 MB.

Or online, if you like

Turn on the online map for streets and buildings everywhere. One switch, off until you choose it.

How it works

The cheapest sure answer wins

Most items are decided by facts in the file. Only what stays uncertain moves on, so AI sees a small share of your library, and you see even less.

  1. 1

    Rules and file signals

    Screenshot markers, WhatsApp names, camera makes, sizes.

  2. 2

    Models on your computer

    Image understanding and text reading, no internet needed.

  3. 3

    A vision model, if you want

    Local through Ollama or llama.cpp, or OpenRouter for cents.

  4. 4

    Your review

    The doubtful rest, most doubtful first.

  5. 5

    Standard tags, then folders

    Every decision is written as metadata first; folders and Immich follow.

AI

Local and free, or cloud for cents

  • Local AI in one click. Roll downloads llama.cpp and Qwen3-VL (about 3 GB) and runs it only while needed. Ollama and LM Studio work too.
  • Ranked by quality. Models are listed with their download size and memory, and Roll names the best one that fits your graphics card.
  • Cloud for cents. OpenRouter with zero data retention: roughly $0.30–2.50 per 100,000 items, with an estimate first and a hard budget cap.
  • Only what's needed leaves. 512 px thumbnails without metadata, only for uncertain items, only when you allow it.
  • Measured, not guessed. The model check compares models on about 150 of your own items before you commit.

Organize

See every move before it happens

Organize shows where every sorted item would go, which folders are new and every name conflict, before a single file moves. Existing files are never overwritten. Stop at any time and Roll continues where it stopped, even after a crash.

Safe by design

Your photos stay yours

Never loses anything

No permanent deletes. Copies are checked against their originals, and every change can be undone from History.

Never re-encodes

Roll changes metadata only, after showing you what changes. Or keep the originals byte for byte and use sidecars.

Works in place

Your folders stay where they are and readable without Roll. Its database can be rebuilt from the tags in the files.

Private by default

Nothing leaves your computer until you turn it on, one switch per kind of traffic. No telemetry. Keys live in the system keychain.

Immich

A curated Immich, kept in sync

Your computer keeps the complete archive. Immich gets what you want on your phone and to share: by default your photos and confirmed events as albums. Later fixes to dates, places and names follow automatically, and private items go only to the Locked folder, only when you pick them.

Get started

  2. Point Roll at your photos

    Pick the folder with your archive and how to save changes. Roll indexes it without changing a file.

FAQ

Questions

Is Roll free?

Yes. Roll is open source under the MIT license, with no account, subscription or ads. Only the optional cloud AI costs money, paid by you to OpenRouter, usually a few cents to a couple of dollars for a whole library, with a hard budget cap.

Does Roll upload my photos?

No. Roll runs on your computer and everything that could reach the internet is off until you turn it on in Settings › Network. If you use cloud AI, only small thumbnails without metadata of the uncertain items are sent, after you have seen the estimate.

Will it change my photos?

Never the pictures themselves. Roll writes metadata (dates, places, tags, names) into the files or into .xmp sidecars, as you choose, and moves files into year folders only after you apply a plan. Every change can be undone.

Do I need Immich?

No. Roll is a complete library on its own. Immich is an optional, self-hosted photo server; if you have one, Roll keeps a curated part of your library in it.

How big a library can it handle?

Roll is built and tested for 100,000 items and more: the timeline and the map stay at 60 frames a second, and indexing continues where it stopped.

Does it run on an Intel Mac or an older Linux?

Not yet. The Mac build needs Apple silicon (M1 or later), and the Linux builds need glibc 2.38 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 39). You can build Roll from source on other systems.

What if I stop using Roll?

Everything Roll knows is in standard metadata in your files and in plain folders. Any photo app, Explorer, Finder or Immich can read them.
